The [overview](/contracts/dao) shows the proposal lifecycle. Here is every
transaction, drawn. The <a href={docUrl("dao/spec.md")}>specification §5</a> owns the exact constraints.

## Step by step

A few conventions make the cards easier to read:

- **Addresses are roles**, not real addresses: `stake_addr`, `proposal_addr`,
  `vote_addr`, `governor_addr` (the settings UTxO), `owner_addr`.
- **Values name tokens by role**: `stakeNFT`, `stakedToken`, `proposalNFT`,
  `voteNFT`, `settingsNFT`, `ADA`.
- **The datum shows types at creation** (`Credential`, `Maybe Credential`, `[]`),
  then leaves unchanged fields blank and shows only what the transaction
  changes. A status shows the constructor it moves to.
- **Validity ranges are symbolic**: `T`, `draft_end`, `voting_end`, `tally_end`.
- **Fee and collateral UTxOs are not drawn.** The validators assert nothing
  about them.

The position **owner** signs for position actions; the **delegatee** cosigns and
votes on its behalf; any sufficiently staked position can propose and vote. The
DAO's parameters are changed through the Settings authority
(`propose_auth`/`apply_auth`). All roles are pluggable credentials — see <a href={docUrl("dao/spec.md")}>spec §3.a</a>.

## Stake positions

### Create stake position

Mints the position NFT and creates the stake UTxO. Ownership is the payment
credential of the spent owner UTxO.

<TxDiagram data={createPosition} />

### Deposit

Adds governance tokens to the position, or only prunes expired locks.

<TxDiagram data={deposit} />

### Withdraw

Withdraws free stake: the total minus the largest live lock.

<TxDiagram data={withdraw} />

### Delegate

Sets, changes, or clears the delegatee. Locks and value pass through untouched.

<TxDiagram data={delegate} />

### Close position

Burns the position NFT once every lock has expired.

<TxDiagram data={closePosition} />

## Proposal lifecycle

### Create proposal

Spends the owner's position (recording a draft lock) and mints the proposal NFT
with the thresholds and timings snapshotted from settings.

<TxDiagram data={createProposal} />

### Cosign

Adds a cosigner's stake to the draft and records their own lock until
`draft_end`.

<TxDiagram data={cosign} />

### Accept draft

Promotes the draft to `Voting` once the cosigning stake meets the accept
threshold. Anyone can do it.

<TxDiagram data={acceptDraft} />

### Reject draft

Burns an unaccepted draft after `draft_end`. The poll never happens.

<TxDiagram data={rejectDraft} />

### End voting stage

Freezes the poll into `Tally` with zeroed counters after `voting_end`.

<TxDiagram data={endVoting} />

### Tally

Consumes vote artifacts in batches, adds their stake to the per-option totals,
burns their NFTs and refunds their lovelace.

<TxDiagram data={tally} />

### End proposal

Closes the poll after `tally_end`. The strict winner's effect runs as a
withdraw-0 when it meets the execute threshold; otherwise nothing executes.

<TxDiagram data={endProposal} />

## Voting

### Vote

Mints a vote artifact weighted by the position's stake and records a lock until
`voting_end`. The proposal is a reference input, not a spend.

<TxDiagram data={vote} />

### Cancel vote

The recorded `stakeOwner` burns the vote NFT at any time. The position's stake
is freed when its lock expires, not at cancel time.

<TxDiagram data={cancelVote} />
