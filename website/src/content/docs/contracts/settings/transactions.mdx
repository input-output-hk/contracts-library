## Step by step

A few conventions make the cards easier to read:

- **Addresses are roles**, not real addresses: `settings_addr`, `applier_addr`.
- **Values name tokens by role**: `settingsNFT`, `ADA`.
- **The datum shows types at creation** (`Data`, `Maybe Data`, `Maybe Int`), then
  leaves unchanged fields blank and shows only what the transaction changes.
- **Validity ranges are symbolic**: `now`, `next_apply`.
- **Fee and collateral UTxOs are not drawn.** The validator asserts nothing about
  them — only about its own input, the seed, its own mint, the `out_ix` output,
  the validity range and the authorization (invariant I8).

`apply_auth` authorizes launch, apply and close; `propose_auth` authorizes
propose. Both are pluggable credentials — see <a href={docUrl("settings/spec.md")}>spec §3.a</a>. Keys authorize by signing; scripts are invoked as a zero-ada withdrawal, which is why no `withdrawals` appear in the cards below.
