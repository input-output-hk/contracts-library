This page summarizes the Event-Triggered Assets specification and includes the
complete usage guide. The canonical documents live in the repository; the full
specification is linked below, and the usage guide is rendered here in full.

## What it is

Event-Triggered Assets is a **tokenized bond** backed by ADA vaults. The issuer
registers the instrument once; anyone can then deposit a fixed bond unit into a
vault and receive a CIP-113 **certificate** for that unit. Each scheduled step
mints a **coupon token** whose asset name is its ADA value. Coupons are redeemed
by burning them at the vault. At maturity the certificate is retired and a
principal coupon is minted.

Issuer triggering is **opt-in**: the owner commits a payment credential with a
self-transfer, after which the issuer can trigger claims and graduation on the
owner's behalf, always paying that credential. The owner keeps the self-service
path.

## Model

| Part             | Meaning                                                                             |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **RegistryNode** | The instrument's entry in the CIP-113 registry.                                     |
| **Vault**        | An ADA UTxO with a vault NFT and a `cnt_policy` datum.                              |
| **Certificate**  | A CIP-113 token at the PLB; its name is the current step.                           |
| **Coupon**       | A native asset under the vault's coupon policy; its name is the amount in lovelace. |
| **Schedule**     | `N` steps on a fixed interval, with baked amounts and principal.                    |

The certificate datum is `{ start, commit, cnt_policy }`. `start` is pinned to
the deposit transaction's validity upper bound, `commit` is `Option<Credential>`
for issuer triggering, and `cnt_policy` selects the vault's coupon terms. The
step lives in the asset name, so every claim burns the current name and mints
the next one.

## Who does what

| Role                   | Who                                | Powers                                                                            |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Holder / depositor** | Anyone                             | Deposit a unit, own/transfer the certificate, claim and redeem coupons, graduate. |
| **Vault owner**        | Company or country                 | Funds the vault off-chain; no on-chain withdrawal path.                           |
| **Issuer**             | Registers the instrument           | Triggers claims and graduation only for committed certificates.                   |
| **Vault**              | ADA UTxO with a validator          | Pays exactly the value of the burned coupons.                                     |
| **Coupon policy**      | Per-vault minting policy           | Mints coupons and admits each scheduled step.                                     |
| **Core protocol**      | CIP-113 PLB, transfer and issuance | Custody and movement of the certificate.                                          |

## What each action does

| Action                | What it does                                                                    |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Register**          | The issuer inserts the RegistryNode once. No certificate is minted.             |
| **Deposit**           | Moves one unit's principal into the vault and mints the certificate at step 0.  |
| **Transfer**          | Moves the certificate; the step, `start` and `cnt_policy` are preserved.        |
| **Authorize**         | Owner self-transfer that sets or clears `commit`.                               |
| **Coupon claim**      | Burns name `k-1`, mints name `k`, and mints the step's coupon.                  |
| **Issuer claim**      | The third-party variant of the coupon claim, paying `commit`.                   |
| **Redemption**        | Burns a coupon at the vault; the vault pays exactly the decoded amount.         |
| **Graduation**        | Burns name `N` and mints the principal-only coupon; the certificate is retired. |
| **Issuer graduation** | The third-party retirement, paying the principal to `commit`.                   |

The owner-signed and issuer-triggered paths are the two certificate lifecycles.
The redemption path is a side branch: a coupon leaves the certificate path and
is burned at the vault for ADA.

## Invariants and assumptions

- Vault solvency is the central assumption. Vaults have no owner withdrawal
  path, but the owner funds them off-chain; an underfunded vault fails
  redemptions until it is topped up.
- Vault creation and funding are outside this design. The deployment assumes the
  authority mints exactly one vault NFT.
- The CIP-113 core is trusted infrastructure.
- Issuer triggering is opt-in and destination-bound: the issuer can only trigger
  committed certificates and must pay the commitment.
- Coupon values live in the asset name; equal amounts are fungible.
- One certificate is handled per transaction; units cannot be merged or split.
- Issuer graduation leaves a certificate-less ghost shell; the owner can sweep
  it later.
- Deposit is permissionless and uncapped; the owner manages total issuance
  off-chain.
