**Linear Vesting** locks a bundle of ada or native tokens for a single
**beneficiary** and releases it continuously between `start_time` and
`end_time`. The beneficiary can claim whatever has vested at any point; the rest
stays locked under the same terms. The schedule is fixed at lock time and never
changes.

After `recovery_time` — always strictly after vesting ends — the **locker** can
recover whatever the beneficiary left unclaimed. Before that, the locker has no
power over the bundle.

## Full lifecycle

Each claim consumes the vesting UTxO and, before `end_time`, returns the
unvested remainder in a continuation with the same datum. Scroll horizontally
to follow the whole cycle.

## Alternative lifecycle: cancel

If the beneficiary leaves funds unclaimed, the locker can end the instance after
`recovery_time` instead of waiting for another claim. `recovery_time` is always
strictly after `end_time`, so cancel can never reach unvested funds.

## When to use it

- Token grants, team allocations, and investor vesting with a fixed schedule.
- Any bundle of ada or native tokens that should unlock linearly over time.
- Cases where a key, multisig, DAO or smart wallet should hold either role —
  both `beneficiary` and `locker` are pluggable credentials.

**When not to:** if you need a cliff, a revocable schedule, or a custom unlock
curve, this linear, immutable contract is not the right shape.

## What this does not protect

The full list is in <a href={docUrl("vesting/spec.md")}>§7 of the specification</a>. The three that change how you deploy:

- **The lock is not validated on-chain.** A malformed lock can strand funds,
  but it can never release more than the schedule allows. Validate the terms in
  your builder.
- **`now` is the validity-range lower bound**, not a wall clock. A builder
  chooses it; the ledger only guarantees inclusion at or after it.
- **Surplus value is not schedule-protected.** Only the assets listed in
  `vesting` are constrained; extra ada or tokens can be swept by the authorized
  beneficiary.
